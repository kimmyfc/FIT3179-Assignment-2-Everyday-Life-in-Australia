Daily averages reveal the quiet weight of personal care, unpaid work and free time. These measures cover the whole population, including people who did not do an activity that day.

THE STORY SO FAR

Everyday life is made
of unequal time and
shared places.

Women spend more time on housework, participation in sport varies by place, and millions take part in cultural events and volunteering. Yet connection and wellbeing are not guaranteed. These datasets describe patterns; they do not establish why those patterns occur.

SOURCES & METHOD

Behind the numbers.

This draft uses the supplied CSV extracts from the ABS and Australian Sports Commission. State maps use simplified 2021 ABS state and territory boundaries; the ACT is shown separately because it is small at national scale. AusPlay source rates are fractions converted to percentages. General Social Survey counts are estimates in thousands. Survey populations and years differ, so charts should be read within their own captions.

Formal volunteering means unpaid work through an organisation in the last 12 months. Cultural attendance means attendance at selected venues or events in the last 12 months. Daily time uses the whole-population average, including non-participants. See each original release for survey notes and reliability guidance.
